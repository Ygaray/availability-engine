<objective>
Restructure the public output contract to the two-list, remaining-capacity shape (D-04); rebase the
domain exception hierarchy onto a typed, closed `ReasonCode` enum (D-03) including a genuinely new
`OutsideHoursError`; and wire both into `engine.py` end-to-end — then freeze the result with a
golden-file conformance test and prove the capacity math with a hypothesis property test.

Purpose: AVAIL-02/AVAIL-04/HOLD-08 all converge on the same three files (contracts, errors, engine)
and must land together — `core/availability.py`'s sweep-line primitive already computes exactly what
D-04 needs (RESEARCH.md verified this by direct read), so this plan's job is exposing that data on
the frozen contract and adding the one genuinely new behavior (outside-hours validation at
`place_hold`, which Phase 1 never performed at all).

Output: `AvailabilityResult.available`/`.booked` with `PublicSlot.capacity`/`.remaining`; a closed
`ReasonCode` StrEnum and `AvailabilityEngineError` hierarchy; `place_hold`'s new outside-hours check;
a committed JSON-schema golden file; a hypothesis property test for the capacity invariant; an
exhaustiveness test proving every exception carries a reason code.
</objective>
